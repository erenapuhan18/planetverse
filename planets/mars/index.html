08Scientific facts

What we know, and how

  • Curiosity found ancient lakebed mudstones with all six elements life requires.
  • MARSIS radar detected bright reflections under the south polar cap, interpreted by some as brine.
  • Perseverance has sealed 20+ samples in tubes, staged for a future return to Earth.
  • InSight recorded over 1,300 marsquakes and measured the core: liquid, and larger than expected.

Fun facts

  • 01Olympus Mons is 22 km tall — nearly three Everests — and as wide as France.
  • 02Valles Marineris would stretch across the entire United States.
  • 03Martian sunsets are blue: fine dust scatters red light away from the Sun's disc.
  • 04Its gravity is so low that a 70 kg astronaut would weigh 26 kg.

09Latest discoveries

The record, in order

Missions: Viking · Spirit & Opportunity · Curiosity · InSight · Perseverance · Ingenuity

  1. 1965

    Mariner 4 returns the first close-up images — a cratered, dead-looking world.

  2. 1976

    Viking 1 and 2 land and run the first biology experiments.

  3. 2012

    Curiosity lands in Gale Crater and confirms an ancient habitable lake.

  4. 2021

    Perseverance lands; Ingenuity makes the first powered flight on another planet.
