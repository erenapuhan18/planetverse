08Scientific facts

What we know, and how

  • The core makes up about 85% of the planet's radius, an iron fraction no other planet matches.
  • MESSENGER radar found billions of tonnes of polar ice, shielded in craters that never see sunlight.
  • Its 3:2 spin-orbit resonance means it turns exactly three times for every two orbits.
  • The magnetic field is offset 480 km north of centre, leaving the southern hemisphere far more exposed.

Fun facts

  • 01A single Mercury day (sunrise to sunrise) lasts two Mercury years.
  • 02Its 600 °C day-to-night temperature swing is the largest of any planet.
  • 03Mercury is shrinking: the iron core cooled and wrinkled the crust into cliffs kilometres high.
  • 04It is the closest planet to Earth on average — because it never strays far from the Sun.
